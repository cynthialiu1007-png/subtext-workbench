# Addendum: RQ3 within E segments (2026-10-29)

Plan reference: analysis plan Section 7 (RQ3). This is an addition. The pre-specified analysis is unchanged and is reported as the primary RQ3 result.

Pre-specified: whether each instability measure and mean confidence predicts run-1 interpretive error across the 48 segments (AUC with 95% bootstrap confidence interval, per model and pooled).

Added: the same analysis restricted to E segments (24 per model), where the outcome is not read (N or M) versus read (F or P); sensitivity analysis with P also counted as not read. Pooled results resample segments as clusters.

Reason: interpretive error is defined differently on E segments (N or M) and C segments (O). If O is rare, errors across the 48 segments come almost entirely from E segments, and any measure that is higher on E segments will predict error; the 48-segment AUC then partly restates the RQ1 comparison of instability between E and C. The within-E analysis asks whether instability tracks failure to read the intended meaning, which is the question interpretive triage depends on.

Status: reported as exploratory and not pre-specified. Written after primary scoring was complete and committed, before any score was linked to a model or to an instability measure, and committed before the response key was read.
