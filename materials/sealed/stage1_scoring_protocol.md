# Stage 1 Scoring Protocol

This protocol specifies how scoring_rubric.docx is applied in Stage 1. The rubric itself is unchanged and remains the protocol for Stage 2. Where this document is silent, the rubric applies.

## 1. Coders and scorers
- In Stage 1, all coding responses are produced by language models. No human coders take part.
- Primary scorer: Xiner Liu (author of the segments and answer key).
- Second scorer: Prof. Xiaoran Hu.
- No model is used to score responses in Stage 1. The model-scoring provision in Section 9 of the rubric does not apply.

## 2. Blinding and order
- Responses are shown to scorers under a random response_id only. Model identity and run number are hidden.
- The key linking response_id to model and run is generated by script and is not opened until all scoring, including the second scorer's, is complete.
- Presentation order is randomised. Scoring proceeds segment by segment, as in Section 9 of the rubric.
- Scorers know the version (E or C) of each segment, as the rubric requires.

## 3. Calibration
- Before formal scoring, both scorers align on the worked examples in Section 6 of the rubric and on at least 10 practice responses.
- Practice responses are drawn from pilot runs. They are excluded from all results.

## 4. Second scoring and agreement
- Prof. Hu independently scores a random sample of at least 20% of responses, without seeing the primary scorer's codes.
- The sample is drawn by script with a recorded random seed.
- Cohen's kappa is computed on pre-discussion scores, as specified in Section 9 of the rubric. If kappa falls below 0.70, scorers recalibrate and rescore.

## 5. Deviations
- Any deviation from this protocol is recorded in a separate, dated file. The sealed files are not modified.
